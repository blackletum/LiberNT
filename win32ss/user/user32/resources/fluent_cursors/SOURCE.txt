Files used, unmodified: cursors/src/svg-white/*.svg; the wait-NN and
progress-NN animation frames are kept in svg/wait/ and svg/progress/.

curgen.py renders each 32-pixel SVG at 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 72, 80, 96 and 128
pixels and writes PNG frames into ../ocr_*.cur, and the 23-frame wait and
progress animations into ../ocr_wait.ani and ../ocr_appstarting.ani at
2 jiffies per frame (30 ms in cursors/src/config). Hotspots come from
cursors/src/config/*.cursor (32-pixel canvas), scaled and rounded down.

    ocr_normal.cur, ocr_icon.cur, ocr_cdautostart.cur   default
    ocr_ibeam.cur                                       text
    ocr_wait.ani                                        wait-01..23
    ocr_cross.cur                                       crosshair
    ocr_up.cur                                          up-arrow
    ocr_sizenwse.cur                                    size_fdiag
    ocr_sizenesw.cur                                    size_bdiag
    ocr_sizewe.cur                                      size_hor
    ocr_sizens.cur                                      size_ver
    ocr_sizeall.cur, ocr_size.cur                       fleur
    ocr_no.cur                                          not-allowed
    ocr_appstarting.ani                                 progress-01..23
    ocr_help.cur                                        help
    ocr_pen.cur                                         pencil
    ocr_hand.cur                                        pointer

Regenerate with: python3 -I curgen.py svg ..
