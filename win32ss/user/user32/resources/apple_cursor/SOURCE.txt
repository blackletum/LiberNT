Files used, unmodified: svg/*.svg (following the repository's symbolic links),
svg/wait/*.svg and svg/left_ptr_watch/*.svg, copied to svg/.

curgen.py applies the macOS colors from the repository's render.json
(#00FF00 to #000000, #0000FF to #FFFFFF) and renders each 256-pixel SVG at the
full cursor size, as configs/win_rg.build.toml does: 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 72,
80, 96 and 128 pixels for the static cursors, 32, 48, 64, 96 and 128 pixels for
the two 40-frame animations, which go into ../ocr_wait.ani and
../ocr_appstarting.ani at 2 jiffies per frame (win_delay 1, doubled by
clickgen). Hotspots come from win_rg.build.toml (sb_up_arrow from
x.build.toml), scaled and truncated as clickgen does, 128,128 when a cursor
has none.
